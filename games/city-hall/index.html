How to play

You are the mayor. Build roads from City Hall, then paint Residential, Commercial and Industrial zones next to roads. Buildings grow on their own when a zone is connected, powered and watered, and demand is there.

Every month taxes come in and services cost money. Watch Happiness: it drives Approval, and Approval below 30% for too long triggers a recall election.

Controls: left-drag paints · right-drag or Bulldoze removes · middle-drag / WASD pans · wheel zooms · hover a tile for info · click a meter on the right to see its overlay.

Milestones at 500 / 2,000 / 10,000 / 50,000 population unlock new buildings and pay a grant.
